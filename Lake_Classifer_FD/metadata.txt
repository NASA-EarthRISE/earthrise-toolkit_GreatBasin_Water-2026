about=about=The Saline Lake Classifier is a QGIS plugin developed for long-term monitoring of saline lakes and wetlands. The plugin accesses Landsat 5 TM, Landsat 7 ETM+, Landsat 8 OLI/TIRS, and Landsat 9 OLI-2/TIRS-2 Collection 2 Level-2 Surface Reflectance imagery through Google Earth Engine, providing observations from 1985 to 2026. It generates true-color imagery, land surface temperature, NDWI, NDVI, NDMI, threshold-based land classifications, and monthly median spectral index values for the summer season. Evapotranspiration is generated using the OpenET Ensemble CONUS GRIDMET Monthly v2.0 dataset, which provides monthly satellite-derived estimates of actual evapotranspiration by combining multiple OpenET models with GRIDMET meteorological data.
